# Reported App Showcase

Style block:
- Canvas: warm off-white `#fff7ef`
- Ink: `#191816`
- Muted text: `#6e6a66`
- Primary action: app orange `#f75d23`
- Secondary signal: app icon green `#0b8a0b`
- Typeface pairing: `Montserrat` for product statements, `IBM Plex Mono` for report metadata.
- Mood: civic utility, fast evidence capture, trustworthy review flow.

Rhythm declaration:
`hook-build-PEAK-breathe-CTA`, 4 scenes across 19 seconds.

Global rules:
- Every scene uses real Reported screenshots as the midground.
- Each screenshot is treated as a moving object inside a device frame, not a static flat image.
- Persistent motifs: orange action rail, green evidence grid, report metadata chips, thin route lines.
- Primary transition: blur crossfade with a slight scale push, 0.55 seconds, `power2.inOut`.
- Accent transition into the close: warmer color wash with the same blur language.

Scene 1, Hook:
- Concept: Reported appears like a civic tool already in motion. The app icon anchors the brand while phone screens glide into a clean reporting workspace.
- Depth layers: warm texture, oversized ghost report labels, app icon, three phone frames, small metadata ticks, orange rail.
- Choreography: logo lifts, headline slides, phone stack cascades, metadata chips count into place.
- Transition out: blur crossfade into the report form.

Scene 2, Evidence to Report:
- Concept: A raw street photo becomes a structured complaint with plate, address, time, and public-facing notes.
- Depth layers: large report form phone, orange callout chips, field connector lines, plate highlight, thin grid.
- Choreography: phone settles with perspective, field chips snap in, connector lines draw, plate value punches forward.
- Transition out: blur crossfade into auto-report.

Scene 3, Auto-Report:
- Concept: Recent photos can be scanned into a bulk review queue, with a user-chosen 4 hr or 7 day lookback window. The app keeps completely private and safe review plus human upload approval at the center.
- Depth layers: auto-report screen, approval checklist, review card, green scan beam, muted background route grid, local music bed.
- Choreography: scan beam sweeps, checklist cascades, scan-window counter rises, approval badge stamps in.
- Transition out: blur crossfade with a warm wash into the close.

Scene 4, CTA:
- Concept: The final frame resolves the workflow into one promise: report, review, submit.
- Depth layers: app icon, screenshot fan, large product name, three action words, orange CTA bar, iPhone/iPad plus Android readiness metadata.
- Choreography: icon scales in, screenshot fan opens, action words land one by one, CTA bar fills.

Negative prompt:
- No fake UI, no synthetic product claims beyond the visible screens, no dark neon tech styling, no generic gradient text, no centered empty slide frames.
