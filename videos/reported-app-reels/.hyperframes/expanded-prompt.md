# Reported Reels 9:16 Production Breakdown

## Style

Format: 1080x1920 vertical video for Reels.
Duration: 19 seconds.

Use the established Reported visual identity from the existing app video:
- Background: #fff7ef
- Foreground: #191816
- Body text: #514d49
- Orange accent: #f75d23
- Green accent: #0b8a0b
- Type: Montserrat for brand and display copy, IBM Plex Mono for plates, counters, and scan labels.

## Rhythm

hook-PUNCH-breathe-CTA.

Scene 1 hooks with the app promise and a large phone. Scene 2 proves structured report fields. Scene 3 slows slightly to establish private scan control. Scene 4 resolves with brand, platform readiness, and a compact CTA.

## Global Rules

- Vertical safe framing: keep core text and UI inside the central 920px width.
- Every scene uses cream background, grain/grid texture, ghost typography, and orange/green glows.
- Transitions are vertical blur-push handoffs, matching Reels scroll momentum.
- No jump cuts; each scene enters with staggered object motion.
- Audio: 19-second polished corporate-tech music bed, 48 kHz stereo AAC.

## Scene 1: Photo To Report

Concept: The viewer sees the Reported app as a fast, focused tool. A phone rises into frame while the headline lands above it: "Turn a photo into a complete report."
Mood: polished civic-tech, clear and energetic, warm but direct.
Depth layers: cream grid, orange glow, green glow, ghost REPORT type, app icon, brand lockup, headline, subcopy, phone, plate/status chips.
Choreography: icon pops, brand slides, headline pushes in, phone lifts and tilts, chips snap into place.
Transition: vertical blur push, 0.5s, power2.inOut.

## Scene 2: Organized Fields

Concept: The messy street moment becomes structured evidence. The report screen anchors the frame while field cards cascade around it.
Mood: precise, administrative, confident.
Depth layers: ghost FIELDS type, phone, plate badge, three field cards, green connectors, orange rule, detail label.
Choreography: phone slides from below, field cards cascade, connectors draw, plate badge stamps into place.
Transition: vertical blur push, 0.5s, power2.inOut.

## Scene 3: Private Scan Window

Concept: Auto-Report is presented as controlled and private, not automatic overreach. The key message is choice: 4 hr or 7 day scan window, completely private and safe, user chooses uploads.
Mood: calmer trust beat, controlled scan energy.
Depth layers: ghost REVIEW type, phone, scan panel, scan beam, three checklist cards, counter pill, privacy chips.
Choreography: panel slides in, beam sweeps, checklist cards drop, counter expands.
Transition: warm blur push, 0.6s, power2.inOut.

## Scene 4: CTA

Concept: Resolve to brand memory. Reported appears with the app icon and stacked devices, then the simple action sequence: Report. Review. Submit.
Mood: decisive, app-store-ready, social CTA.
Depth layers: ghost SUBMIT type, app icon, two phones, iPad hint, CTA words, platform badge, closing bar.
Choreography: icon pops, devices fan in, words stamp, platform badge slides, scene fades at the end.

## Negative Prompt

Do not stretch the horizontal layout. Do not use small web-sized text. Do not add decorative purple/blue gradients. Do not use fake app screens. Do not use external stock footage. Keep all copy readable without audio.
